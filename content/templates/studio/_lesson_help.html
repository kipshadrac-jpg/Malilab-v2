How to format a lesson (short paragraphs, subheadings, colour)

Keep paragraphs to 2–4 lines with a blank line between them. Learners can still highlight and add notes on each part.

  • ## Subheading and ### Smaller subheading: each main subheading gets its own colour.
  • **bold** for the key point, ==highlight== for yellow, or ==green:text==, ==blue:text==, ==pink:text==, ==orange:text==.
  • - item for bullets, 1. item for numbered steps.
  • > tip: text for a coloured box. Kinds: tip, warning, key, example, note.
  • Tables: | Option | Rate | then | Sacco | 8% | (first row is the heading row).
