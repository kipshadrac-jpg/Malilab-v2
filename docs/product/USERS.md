# Users and Contexts

## Audience

MaliLab serves working and aspiring working Kenyans across income levels and occupations. The master specification gives these examples, not exclusive personas:

- School-going young people interested in money
- University and college students
- Employed young adults
- Informal workers, including boda boda riders
- Mama mboga and other small traders
- Freelancers and professionals
- Entrepreneurs and business owners
- Corporate executives
- People preparing for retirement

## Design implications

Do not assume that every user has a formal salary, bank account, investment experience, high disposable income, or advanced financial knowledge. Explanations and practical scenarios should be capable of adapting to different financial realities.

Playbooks should let people recognize their situation, question, ambition, or problem. Lessons should use accessible explanations, examples, and Kenyan context. Practice should let the user explore their own circumstances and assumptions.
