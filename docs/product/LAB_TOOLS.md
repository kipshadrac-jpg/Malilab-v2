# Lab Tools

## Purpose

Lab Tools are practical research tools, including calculators where appropriate, in the initial core loop. They help users quantify and explore a personally defined financial-freedom target using their own circumstances and assumptions. They support Practice and My Plan; they do not provide guaranteed financial advice or outcomes.

Calculators should allow exploration of relevant variables such as current income, desired income, savings or contributions, timeframe, investment assumptions, inflation, changes in income, and other relevant variables. This is a set of examples, not a final mandatory input list for every calculator.

## Potential categories

The master specification lists savings projections, compound growth, contribution scenarios, target-date calculations, income replacement scenarios, inflation scenarios, investment-return sensitivity, debt/repayment scenarios, and emergency-fund scenarios. These are potential categories; no final catalogue is approved.

## Output expectations

Tools must clearly show inputs, assumptions, formulas or methodology where appropriate, outputs, sensitivity/scenario changes, limitations, and educational context. Hypothetical calculations must not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.
