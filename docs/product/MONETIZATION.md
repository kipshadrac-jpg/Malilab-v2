# Monetization

## Confirmed direction

MaliLab is intended to become a monthly subscription service in the future. Subscription and payment implementation are not part of the initial product scope. The initial product phase should be free so the owner can observe how people use MaliLab, what content attracts users, what they value, where they stop, which Playbooks perform well, and which Modules create meaningful engagement.

Advanced monetization is future scope. At the product-planning level, future architecture should avoid making subscription or entitlement support impossible. This does not select an architecture or authorize payment processing now. Advanced analytics for evaluating the initial phase are also future scope unless separately approved; candidate product signals do not themselves authorize analytics collection.
