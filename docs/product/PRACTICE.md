# Practice

## Purpose

Practice is where learning becomes a “Lab”: users connect knowledge to their own situation through exploratory questions, activities, and, where appropriate, Lab Tools.

Practice should help a user define and investigate what financial freedom means for their own circumstances and quantify a personally chosen target. Possible prompts include desired income, current resources, realistic contributions, time to a target, assumptions affecting an outcome, and Kenyan instruments that could potentially support a strategy. These prompts support exploration and do not impose a universal definition of financial freedom.

## Relationship to learning and planning

Practice follows or accompanies learning and may use a Lab Tool. It can inform Notes and a user's attempt to build My Plan. It is exploratory and does not guarantee a financial result or prescribe a personal decision.

## Safety

Clearly distinguish educational information, hypothetical scenarios, calculations, assumptions, and personal financial decisions. Do not turn illustrative scenarios into promises. Make uncertainty and limitations apparent where relevant.
