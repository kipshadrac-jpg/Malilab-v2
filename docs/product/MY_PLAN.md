# My Plan

## Purpose

My Plan is the user's personal planning workspace and part of the initial core loop. It grows from learning and gives the user a place to define and investigate what financial freedom means in their circumstances, including a target, timeframe, assumptions, and possible path. It is exploratory, not a promise or guarantee.

Financial freedom has no universal MaliLab definition. A user may define it as replacing employment income, reaching desired monthly income, becoming debt-free, building sufficient assets, leaving an occupation, reaching a lifestyle target, creating income independence, or another personally relevant target. These are examples only.

The intended conceptual flow is:

**Lesson → Insight → Note → My Plan → Lab Tool → Personal Scenario**

## Potential contents

The initial My Plan should support the user's target, timeframe, assumptions, and possible path, along with the minimum context needed to investigate it. The user's plan attempt and its target and assumptions should persist with the account sufficiently to support Progress and learning achievement. It may eventually hold richer goals, income information, savings/investment assumptions, multiple scenarios, notes, decisions, selected tools, and learning references. This does not establish every potential field as an initial-release requirement.

## Relationship to Practice

Practice and Lab Tools can help users explore questions and assumptions relevant to My Plan. A meaningful learning achievement combines Module completion with a practical plan attempt. Conceptually, an attempt constructs and investigates the user's target, timeframe, assumptions, and possible path. Detailed thresholds and evidence representation remain TODO/TO-BE-DEFINED; the product should preserve the plan attempt and its relevant learning context.

## Safety and user control

Plans and calculations must not be presented as guaranteed outcomes. The user’s circumstances and assumptions matter. Plan information persists with the account for the core loop. TODO/TO-BE-DEFINED: privacy, editing, export, deletion, sharing, and detailed data retention requirements.
