# Notes

## Purpose

Notes are a minimum-scope, context-aware learning capability in the initial product. They connect:

**What I learned → What I think → What I plan**

They are not intended to become a generic, disconnected note-taking app.

## Confirmed capabilities in concept

Users should be able to highlight useful information, save an insight, write a note, connect a note to a Lesson, and potentially send an insight into My Plan. “Potentially” leaves the exact transfer behavior open.

## Relationships

Notes can be associated with Lesson learning and may inform My Plan. They support the transition from study to personal reflection and planning. Notes should persist with the user's account sufficiently to support this core loop. This is a product requirement, not a decision about storage or data model.
