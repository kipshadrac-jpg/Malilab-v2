# Playbooks

## Purpose

A Playbook is the primary discovery and marketing entry point. It starts from a realistic financial situation, question, ambition, or problem that allows a person to recognize themselves. It explores the situation rather than simply supplying a financial answer.

Playbooks should attract discovery/search traffic, create curiosity, explain what needs to be understood, connect users to relevant Modules, introduce practical experiments, lead toward personal planning, and recommend related Playbooks naturally. Playbook-led discovery is part of initial core scope; basic search is included where required by the approved UX. A sophisticated recommendation engine is future scope.

## Possible content

A Playbook may include a long-form cover, title, short description, scenario, starting assumptions, financial context, investigation questions, relevant Modules, practical experiments, Lab Tools, related Playbooks, and possible next steps. This list is flexible: not every Playbook must use every section or the same number/order of sections.

## Example from the master specification

A 20-year-old boda boda rider earns approximately KES 800 per working day and wants to stop depending on boda boda work by age 32. This is an illustrative scenario, not a verified typical income, financial claim, recommended plan, or promise of an outcome.

## Content and safety

Use Kenyan context, distinguish scenario assumptions from facts, and avoid guaranteeing outcomes. Where current regulatory, tax, rate, product availability, or institutional information appears, the content system should support attribution and review.
