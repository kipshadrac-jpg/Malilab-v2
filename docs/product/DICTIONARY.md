# MaliLab Dictionary

## Purpose

The Dictionary is a future core knowledge feature explaining Kenyan financial terminology in accessible language. Entries should be linkable from Lessons and Playbooks.

Illustrative terms include CDS account, Treasury Bill, Treasury Bond, yield, coupon, SACCO, MMF, and CRB. This list is not exhaustive or a final taxonomy.

## Knowledge connections

Dictionary terms can clarify concepts in Lessons and Playbooks and contribute to a cross-linked knowledge environment that may also include instruments, institutions, concepts, laws or regulations where appropriate, strategies, and scenarios. The Dictionary taxonomy is explicitly TODO/TO-BE-DEFINED.

## Content quality

Use accessible explanations and Kenyan context. Where entries contain current regulatory, tax, rate, availability, or institutional information, support source attribution and content review. Avoid unsupported claims or false certainty.
