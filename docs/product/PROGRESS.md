# Progress and Learning Completion

## Product intent

Progress should reflect exploration and applied learning, not only reading or click completion. The intended progression can include Playbook discovery, Module selection, Lessons studied, Practice experiment, Module completion, plan attempt, possible Badge, and further exploration. It should support flexible exploration rather than enforce a rigid single-course path.

## Meaningful learning achievement

The specified concept combines:

**Module completion + practical plan attempt**

This represents studying a structured financial topic and attempting to apply the knowledge to a personal situation. Badges can represent successful advancement. The exact completion criteria, evidence, and badge system are explicitly undefined.

Conceptually, **Module completion** means completing the intended learning for that Module; it is not merely a click or reading event. A **practical plan attempt** means the user has constructed and investigated a personal financial-freedom target, timeframe, assumptions, and possible path based on their circumstances. This is an attempt to define and explore a direction, not a claim that the target is achievable or achieved.

The product should preserve enough evidence to show both parts of the achievement: which intended Module learning was completed, and that the user made a plan attempt with their defined target and assumptions. Where applicable, this evidence should retain context connecting the attempt to relevant Lessons, Practice, or Lab Tools. Detailed thresholds, representation, and mechanics remain TODO/TO-BE-DEFINED.

The initial product should support a minimal persistent progress/achievement record for the user. This does not define account implementation or data structure.

## Product signals

Potential signals listed in the master specification are Playbook discovery and engagement, Module starts, Lesson completion, Practice attempts, Lab Tool usage, My Plan creation, Module completion, plan attempts, Badges earned, related Playbook exploration, return usage, and content usefulness. These are candidate signals, not approved KPIs or a permission to collect data. Final KPIs await explicit approval.
