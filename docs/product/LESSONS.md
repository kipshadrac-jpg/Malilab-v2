# Lessons

## Purpose

Lessons are the primary learning units within Modules. They explain financial knowledge in an accessible, practical way grounded in Kenyan context.

## Reading experience

Prioritize skimmability, short paragraphs, strong hierarchy, meaningful spacing, frequent subheadings, bold key insights, appropriate lists, clear examples, visual organization, and connections to other concepts. MaliLab is an editorial reading and interactive learning platform. The curated book/story/library reference describes organization and experience only; it does not imply audio functionality.

There is no speaker button, voice conversation, text-to-speech, podcast/audio lesson requirement, voice learning mode, or audio-first lesson format.

Editorial guidance favors short 2–4 line paragraphs, whitespace, clear progression, practical application, and accessible explanations. Avoid jargon without explanation, giant text walls, generic US assumptions, unsupported claims, false certainty, guaranteed outcomes, and excessive marketing language.

## Learning connections

A Lesson belongs to a Module and may connect to Practice activities, Lab Tools, Dictionary terms, concepts, and context-aware Notes. The exact content block format and interaction behavior are not defined in Product Definition.
