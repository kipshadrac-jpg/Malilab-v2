# Relationship Catalogue

Cardinalities describe the logical model, not table syntax. “Delete behavior” is conceptual and subject to the retention policy in DATA_LIFECYCLE.md. Editorial relations are revision-aware wherever edits could change a learner's historical context.

## Relationship design rules

- Playbook recommendations belong to Playbook Revision and point to exact Module Revision. This supports many-to-many discovery without per-Playbook Module copies.
- Lesson membership/order/classification belongs to Module Revision through Module Learning Requirement. A Lesson identity is reusable but reuse is optional editorially.
- Historical user records hold references to exact editorial revisions, not a copy of editorial body. Small scenario/assumption context may be snapshotted when necessary to explain the user's own path.
- Cross-domain references do not transfer ownership. Every user-to-user-data edge must verify a common owner where both endpoints are private.
- Circular domain dependency is forbidden: Progress consumes evidence from Learning/Practice/Plan; My Plan does not require Progress.
